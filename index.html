01 /The problem

Why most AI spending doesn't pay off.

No. 1

Tokens add up quietly

The biggest model gets used for small jobs, prompts are bloated and nobody sets limits. The bill grows and the results don't.

No. 2

Tools nobody opens

Someone builds something impressive that doesn't fit how the team works. A month later it sits unused.

No. 3

Prompts that don't work twice

Everyone types their own. Results vary, nothing is reusable, and no one knows what a good answer looks like.

I fix all three, and I'll tell you when AI isn't the answer.
